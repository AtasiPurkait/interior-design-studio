Our Philosophy

Coloring Dreams, Designing Spaces

For over fifteen years, A.P. Interior & Home Decoration has stood as a beacon of artisanal integrity and contemporary refinement in Eastern India.

What began as a specialized residential design practice in Kolkata has evolved into an award-winning architectural and interior studio. Our multidisciplinary collective of architects, interior stylists, lighting consultants, and master artisans approach every space as a blank canvas—weaving light, texture, proportion, and bespoke materials into spaces that breathe.

We eliminate the anxiety of interior renovations by taking 100% turnkey accountability: from custom 3D spatial planning, German modular cabinetry fabrication, and plumbing reconfiguration to the final Italian marble polish and white-glove handover in exactly 45 business days.

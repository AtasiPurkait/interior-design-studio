Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to common questions about timelines, warranty coverage, materials, and payment milestones.

Once your 3D design plans and material selections are frozen and the site is civil-ready, our factory begins automated laser fabrication. Onsite modular assembly and finishing are completed within 45 business days. If we delay, we pay you a penalty fee per day as per our written contract.

Our 10-year warranty covers all structural integrity of cabinetry, boiling water resistant (BWR/BWP) marine ply against termites and borer, hinges and soft-close drawer runners (Blum / Hettich), and delamination of factory-pressed acrylics or laminates.

Every home we design is 100% custom and bespoke! While we leverage precision German factory modularity for kitchen and wardrobe carcasses for millimeter-perfect joints, all facades, living room credenzas, accent stone walls, wall paneling, and ceiling designs are tailored uniquely to your floor plan and aesthetic vision.

Absolutely! We welcome you to visit our Kolkata Experience Center to touch and feel our full library of Italian marble, solid teak, acrylic shutters, quartz counters, and smart lighting systems. We can also arrange a walkthrough of ongoing or completed residences in your neighborhood upon client consent.

We follow a transparent 4-stage payment model: 5% initial booking to initiate 3D photorealistic renderings; 45% upon 3D plan approval to begin factory fabrication; 40% when factory panels arrive on site for assembly; and the remaining 10% only after final white-glove inspection and your full satisfaction.
